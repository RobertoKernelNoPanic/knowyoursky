Updates

Changelog board for KnowYourSky, in plain language.

Version 1.0.73

October 2026
  • Fixed a bug where the level timer started counting already during the intro screens and tutorial, instead of only when the real level began
  • Reorganized mode selection and other menus into tidier, more compact cards that fill the screen with no scrolling needed
  • "Hand guide" is now called "Find with your hands": no more scrolling list — instead, cards with a small preview of each constellation's shape (now always properly centered); opening one shows the constellation large with a compass pointing the way (if enabled, otherwise it asks on the spot)
  • Added a skippable mini-tutorial in "Find with your hands" on how to use it outdoors (hold the phone flat, move around horizontally)
  • Polished app icon: the alien is shown from behind, in a more adult style with a touch of shading for depth, pointing at a small glowing Orion

Version 1.0.72

October 2026
  • Added a compass (optional, off by default, enable it in Settings) to "Hand guide" mode: shows a live "turn this way" indicator for each constellation, both on the dedicated screen and the in-game button — no location data used or saved, just the phone's magnetometer
  • Added a skippable mini-tutorial the first time you open "Hand guide", reachable again any time with the "?" button
  • New city picker in Settings: choose from a fixed list which city to use for real-sky calculations ("Real time" and "Hand guide") — no GPS, just this choice
  • New app icon: the game's own alien character, pointing at a constellation in the sky

Version 1.0.71

October 2026
  • New "Hand guide" mode (beta): live directions to find tonight's principal constellations outdoors, using nothing but your own hand as a ruler (fists and handspans)
  • Added a small button in "Real time" mode to get the same directions for the current question, without giving away the answer
  • Added small transition animations in menus and in-game (button presses, screen changes) for a smoother feel
  • Fixed a bug where a bottom panel during gameplay could overlap Android's navigation bar on some devices
  • Added a small hint whenever a screen has more content to scroll to further down, so it's no longer easy to miss

Version 1.0.70

September 2026
  • Fixed a tutorial bug: if you dragged the view and then tapped "Next", the following step could show up off-center or at the wrong zoom level — every step now always starts with the right framing
  • Allowed a bit more zoom, to better see the color of smaller stars

Version 1.0.69

September 2026
  • Before the very first level, the app now asks whether you want to see the tutorial or skip straight to playing — it no longer starts automatically
  • Cleaned up the end-of-level screen: score, bonus, and time now sit in small icon cards instead of a long list of text

Version 1.0.68

September 2026
  • The tutorial becomes a real game mode, "Interactive": it's in the mode picker and can be played any time, not just the first time
  • On replay, it teaches a different constellation at random from five (Orion, Cygnus, Taurus, Leo, Gemini), not always the same one
  • Added three animated segments with the classic reference points for getting oriented in the sky: the Big Dipper's pointer stars to the North Star, the Dipper's arc to Arcturus, and Orion's belt to Sirius — with the view actually gliding from one star to the other
  • The tutorial's hands-on challenges now only ask about things worth checking (color, brightness) — no more "tap anything" steps
  • New "Difficulty levels, explained" page, available any time from the mode picker
  • Small text fix on the guide screen

Version 1.0.67

September 2026
  • New interactive tutorial before your first level: 23 steps, with explanations and small hands-on challenges, to learn to recognize stars and constellations from scratch — colors, brightness, star names, the North Star, objects like nebulae and clusters, and how to actually get oriented in the sky
  • The tutorial can be replayed any time from Settings
  • Fixed difficulty selection on the guide screen: it now actually works, instead of always staying on "Expert" no matter what you tap

Version 1.0.66

September 2026
  • Fixed the app icon: Orion's orientation now matches the real sky
  • Simplified the home screen's sky map: removed the view-mode switch, it now always shows the correct representation (equatorial, as on star charts)

Version 1.0.65

September 2026
  • Fixed an orientation bug: some constellations (Orion included) appeared mirrored compared to the real sky, both in-game and in the app icon
  • Clearer difficulty rules: Easy has no bonus rounds, Default asks about the brightest stars, Expert gives no help after answering
  • Score bonus for playing Medium/Expert difficulty with guide lines off, now visible at the end of a run and on the leaderboard
  • Two new guide screens before your first level: how scoring works, and what changes between difficulty levels
  • Nearby constellation names now appear after every answer, to help you learn to spot them
  • Redesigned, more modern home screen
  • New tagline "Don't just point at the sky. Actually learn it." on the home screen and app launch
